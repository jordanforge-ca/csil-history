# Experiment 001 — ECPM manual identity and dates

Control: [csil-history#14](https://github.com/jordanforge-ca/csil-history/issues/14).  
Parent research area: [csil-history#5](https://github.com/jordanforge-ca/csil-history/issues/5).  
Run: GrokBot researcher, run 1 of 3. 20 September 2026.

## Question

What does publicly accessible evidence establish about the identity and relationship of the c. 1990 ECPM *Skill Development Manual* and the reported c. 1994 ECPM training manual?

As far as this bounded search allows:

1. Strongest independently verifiable bibliographic details for the 1990 ECPM manual.
2. Independently verifiable evidence for a distinct 1994 ECPM training manual.
3. Whether the evidence supports one document with inconsistent dates, different editions/revisions, distinct works, or not enough evidence.
4. Whether either document is publicly accessible in full text or has a reliable library/archive catalogue location.

This run does **not** ask whether ECPM formally preceded CSIL.

## Scope and limits

Started from the existing catalogue (`sources/wanted-ecpm-manuals.qmd`, Roeher/Salisbury finding-aid record, Van der Klift 1994 near-miss, `references.bib` `ecpm1990manual`). Did not restart the ECPM landscape or write ECPM→CSIL narrative.

Hard ceilings from #14 were treated as stop conditions. Native interfaces that blocked or timed out were **not** worked around by contacting libraries or people. No living participant was contacted. No paywalled collection was opened. No copyright judgment about uploading a scan was required, because no manual text was recovered.

## Evidence for the 1990 item

The strongest independently verifiable bibliographic description found in this run is a single late-1990s annotated-bibliography entry. Quoted in full as it appears in the inspected Roeher/Salisbury PDF:

> BC Rehab & Greater Vancouver Home Support Society  
> “Enhanced Consumer Participation Model: Skill Development Manual for Consumers of Home Support Services”, BC Rehab & Greater Vancouver Home Support Society, 1990  
> A training manual that includes 5 modules intended to empower consumers to act as effective employers (52 pages plus appendices)

Independently verifiable from that inspected entry:

| Element | As cited | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title | Enhanced Consumer Participation Model: Skill Development Manual for Consumers of Home Support Services | Finding-aid wording only |
| Corporate authors | BC Rehab & Greater Vancouver Home Support Society | Repeated as author and as publisher-like attribution; no personal names |
| Date | 1990 | Year only; no month, edition, or place |
| Form | “A training manual” with “5 modules” | Annotation, not a table of contents |
| Extent | “52 pages plus appendices” | Annotation; appendices not enumerated |
| Purpose (as annotated) | “intended to empower consumers to act as effective employers” | Roeher/Salisbury wording, not the unread manual |

What that entry does **not** supply: ISBN, ISSN, OCLC number, Microlog number, place of publication, personal authors, editor, edition statement, module titles, or a library location.

No second independent bibliography, library holding, or contemporaneous 1990 instrument repeating those details was found in this bounded pass. Later project pages that name “ECPM” without this title are later organizational wording, not a second bibliographic confirmation of the 1990 manual.

## Evidence for the reported 1994 item

Two in-project statements report a possible 1994 training item. Both were inspected. Neither cites a title page, catalogue record, or contemporaneous bibliography.

1. **Commissioning issue** ([jordanforge-infrastructure#10](https://github.com/jordanforge-ca/jordanforge-infrastructure/issues/10)) — working research proposition only: “ECPM material around 1990 and possibly a 1994 revision/training manual”; wanted-source line “c. 1990 and possibly a 1994 training revision.” Emma Van der Klift is listed among people the site structure should accommodate; the issue does **not** itself attach her name to a 1994 manual title page.
2. **Parent research brief** ([csil-history#5](https://github.com/jordanforge-ca/csil-history/issues/5)) — uncited statement: “Evidence suggests ECPM material ~1990 and a ~1994 training manual involving BC Rehab, Vancouver Health Department, Greater Vancouver Home Support Society, Emma Van der Klift.” No source is given for that cluster.

Public searches in this run for a 1994 ECPM training manual, and for Van der Klift + ECPM / “Enhanced Consumer Participation” / home-support / BC Rehab, did not return a second titled work.

The one 1994 Van der Klift publication actually opened — “Hell-Bent on Helping,” originally in Thousand, Villa and Nevin, *Creativity and Collaborative Learning* (Paul Brookes, 1994); Broadreach republication inspected — is about help, friendship, and inclusive education. It does not name ECPM, CSIL, BC Rehab, Greater Vancouver Home Support Society, or Vancouver Health Department. It is a **near-miss**, not bibliographic evidence for a 1994 ECPM manual.

The Roeher/Salisbury finding aid lists the 1990 skill-development manual and does **not** list a 1994 ECPM revision. Other 1994 items in that bibliography are different works (for example Salisbury essays and a Roeher Institute paper). Those 1994 dates must not be read as ECPM-manual dates.

IFRC and Gauthier materials seen as snippets name ECPM as a later-remembered project and give CSIL start-year wording. They do not independently verify a 1994 *training manual* title, author, extent, or holding. They are out of scope for the predecessor question and are noted only to record that they do not fill the 1994-manual gap.

## Comparison / what can and cannot be concluded

Can be said:

- A late-1990s public finding aid cites one 1990 training manual with a specific title, corporate authors, five modules, and “52 pages plus appendices.”
- A distinct 1994 ECPM training manual is **reported** in later project issues, without a cited 1990s source.
- One inspected 1994 Van der Klift publication exists and is a different work.
- No public full text and no reliable holding record for either sought manual was retrieved in this run.

Cannot be said from this evidence:

- That the 1994 report is the same physical document as the 1990 Roeher-cited manual.
- That a 1994 item is a revised edition of the 1990 manual.
- That two distinct ECPM manuals were issued.
- That Emma Van der Klift or Vancouver Health Department appear on any unread title page.
- That ECPM formally preceded CSIL (explicitly out of scope; later project pages that say so were not used to answer this question).

Repeating the Roeher citation on later catalogue pages is not independent confirmation.

## Contradictions or ambiguities

- **1990 vs 1994** is not yet a contradiction between two inspected bibliographic records. It is one inspected 1990 citation versus later uncited project reports of a possible 1994 training item.
- **#10 vs #5:** #10 says “possibly a 1994 revision/training manual.” #5 states that “evidence suggests” a ~1994 training manual involving a named cluster of organizations and Emma Van der Klift, without showing that evidence.
- **Author vs publisher:** Roeher lists “BC Rehab & Greater Vancouver Home Support Society” twice and gives no personal authors. Whether those bodies authored, published, or commissioned the unread manual cannot be settled from the finding aid alone.
- **“BC Rehab” vs “BC Rehabilitation Society”:** the finding aid uses “BC Rehab”; the catalogue already treats that as the same organization. This run did not inspect a title page that would confirm the printed form.
- **Training manual vs skill-development manual:** Roeher’s annotation calls the 1990 item “a training manual.” That wording alone cannot confirm or deny a separately titled 1994 “training” work.
- Search-engine summaries sometimes restated the Roeher entry as if a second source had been found, or pointed at unrelated attendant-training pages. Those summaries were discarded; only inspected pages count.

## Conclusion with confidence

**INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE**

Publicly accessible evidence in this bounded run is enough to treat a 1990 *Enhanced Consumer Participation Model: Skill Development Manual for Consumers of Home Support Services* (BC Rehab & Greater Vancouver Home Support Society; five modules; 52 pages plus appendices) as a **bibliographically attested, unread** publication, on the strength of one inspected late-1990s finding aid. It is **not** enough to confirm or reject a distinct 1994 ECPM training manual, an edition/revision relationship, or a single document with conflicting dates. No public full text and no reliable library/archive holding record was retrieved for either sought item.

## Unresolved questions

- Does a second, independent 1990s citation or a library holding exist for the Roeher-cited 1990 title (UBC/SFU/LLBC/LAC Aurora still unchecked as native catalogues)?
- Is there any contemporaneous source—not a 2026 research issue—for a 1994 ECPM training manual?
- If a 1994 item exists, is it a revision of the 1990 manual, a different title, or a later mis-dating of the 1990 work?
- What personal names, if any, appear on the 1990 title page?
- Do Vancouver Health Department or Emma Van der Klift appear on any ECPM manual, or only in later research notes?
- Where did a circulating copy go (Roeher/IRIS files, GVHSS successor, VCH, provincial library deposit)?

Those questions would need either a recovered copy, a holding record, or (if still missing) a later authorized expansion. They are not answered here.
