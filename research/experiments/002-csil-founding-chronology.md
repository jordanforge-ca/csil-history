# Experiment 002 — CSIL founding chronology (1993 / 1994 / 1995)

## Question

What does the publicly accessible evidence establish about the meaning of **1993**, **1994**, and **1995** in the founding chronology of Choice in Supports for Independent Living (CSIL) in British Columbia?

## Scope and limits

This run follows control issue [#17](https://github.com/jordanforge-ca/csil-history/issues/17). It answers only the chronology-meaning question. It does **not** identify a design committee, assign founder credit, prove ECPM→CSIL causation, reconstruct early policy detail, or contact living participants.

Method constraints observed: no repository-wide grep/code search; no nested agents; catalogue-first; exact artifact audit below.

## Evidence for 1993

| Source | Type | What it says (as recorded in catalogue) | Meaning supported? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gauthier 2014 SCI presentation | Later recollection | “Established by the Ministry of Health … in **1993**”; also “The Pilot — 50 people tried it out.” | Labels **establishment** in 1993. Does not supply a ministry instrument. Same speaker uses **1994** in 2015. |

**Conclusion for 1993:** The only support in this bound is later participant/organizational recollection. It does **not** establish a distinct 1993 milestone (design approval vs pilot intake vs other) on contemporaneous or later-official evidence opened here.

## Evidence for 1994

| Source | Type | What it says | Meaning supported? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ombudsperson *Best of Care* Part 2 (2012) | Later official | “established as a pilot in **1994** and implemented province-wide in 1995”; also “created as a pilot project” in 1994; elsewhere “introduced in 1994.” | Assigns **pilot / introduction** to 1994 as part of a two-stage official chronology. |
| Gauthier 2015 Module 1 | Later recollection | “Established … in **1994**” | Uses “established” for 1994 (conflicts with same speaker’s 2014 “1993”). |
| IFRC Our Team | Later organizational | Gauthier “started on the CSIL program in **1994**” | Personal/program start year on org page; not a ministry instrument. |

**Conclusion for 1994:** Later official reconstruction supports treating 1994 as a **pilot / introduction** year. Later recollection also uses 1994 for “established,” which collides with the 2014 “1993” wording and does not by itself define a stage.

## Evidence for 1995

| Source | Type | What it says | Meaning supported? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ombudsperson 2012 | Later official | “implemented province-wide in **1995**” | Assigns **province-wide implementation** to 1995. |
| HEU *Guardian* Nov/Dec 1995 | Contemporaneous | CSIL is a “**new** program” of Community Support Branch / Continuing Care Division, “**Designed as a pilot program**”; ~130 clients among ~65,000 continuing-care clients. | Confirms CSIL existed publicly in late 1995 as ministry-housed and still described as pilot/new. Does **not** date the start to 1993/94/95 and does **not** independently confirm a completed province-wide rollout. |

**Conclusion for 1995:** Later official reconstruction supports a **province-wide** milestone in 1995. Contemporaneous HEU late-1995 wording still frames CSIL as new/pilot with a small caseload — so “province-wide in 1995” is **not** independently confirmed by that contemporaneous source and must not be collapsed into a settled fact.

## Cross-source comparison

| Year | Later official (Ombudsperson) | Contemporaneous (HEU 1995) | Later recollection (Gauthier/IFRC) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1993 | Not used | Not used | “Established” (2014 only) |
| 1994 | Pilot / created / introduced | Not dated | “Established” (2015); “started … in 1994” (IFRC) |
| 1995 | Province-wide implementation | Still “new/pilot”; ~130 clients | Not decisive here |

The 2008 Program Review Synthesis (inspected catalogue record) dates establishment only to “the 1990's” and **does not choose** 1993 vs 1994 — useful negative evidence against using that review to settle the start year.

Original 1993–95 Ministry of Health design/pilot instruments remain **wanted** (`sources/wanted-ministry-csil-pilot-records.qmd`).

## What can and cannot be concluded

**Can (within this bound):**

- A **later official** chronology assigns different *meanings* to 1994 (pilot) and 1995 (province-wide).
- A **contemporaneous** late-1995 source still calls CSIL a new/pilot program and names Community Support Branch / Continuing Care Division — supporting presence and pilot framing in 1995, not the Ombudsperson’s province-wide completion claim by itself.
- **1993** appears only as a later “established” label that conflicts with the same speaker’s later “1994” wording.

**Cannot:**

- Treat 1993 as a verified distinct stage (design vs approval vs intake).
- Treat “established,” “pilot,” “introduced,” and “province-wide” as already reconciled synonyms.
- Claim province-wide 1995 is contemporaneously proven.
- Reconstruct authorization instruments, design-group membership, or ECPM formal succession.

## Final classification with confidence

**PARTIAL STAGED CHRONOLOGY** — **moderate confidence for the classification choice; low confidence that the stages are historically settled.**

Reason: The evidence supports treating **1994** and **1995** as *candidate* stages with different official meanings (pilot vs province-wide) in a 2012 oversight report, while **1993** remains an unresolved conflicting start-year label rather than a evidenced third stage. Contemporaneous 1995 material keeps pilot language alive and blocks upgrading the 1995 province-wide claim to verified-inspected fact without original instruments.

Not chosen:

- **STAGED CHRONOLOGY SUPPORTED** — would overstate certainty on 1995 province-wide and on clear meanings for all three years.
- **CONFLICTING START-YEAR LABELS ONLY** — understates that later official wording *does* assign different verbs to 1994 vs 1995.
- **SINGLE START YEAR SUPPORTED** — not justified.
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** — too weak; the bound does establish a usable partial pattern plus explicit gaps.

## Unresolved questions / original-record needs
